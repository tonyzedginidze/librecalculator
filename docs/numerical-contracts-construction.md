# Construction quantity numerical contracts

All inputs are canonical ungrouped decimals, no exponent notation, using the common parser (30 significant input digits, 50 working digits). Dimensions, area and coverage are positive from 0.000000000001 through 1000000 in the stated units; no hidden unit conversion is inferred. Allowances are 0–100%. Integer counts and coats are ASCII digits from 1–100. Results outside adjusted exponent magnitude 100 fail. Calculations do not use binary floating arithmetic. Comparisons use the same result's unadjusted reference and answer; differing units are explicitly disclosed. Quantities are educational planning estimates, not structural design or installation advice.

- **Concrete**: rectangular slab, dimensions in metres. `L × W × D × (1+w/100)`, output m³ without purchase rounding. Fixtures `(5,4,.1,10%) →2.2`; `(1,1,1,0%) →1`; `(2,3,.2,5%) →1.26`. Wet bag yield and structural thickness are not inferred.
- **Paint**: `net m² × integer coats ÷ m²/L coverage × (1+w/100)`, output litres before can rounding. Fixtures `(40,2,10,10%) →8.8`; `(12,1,6,0%) →2`; `(30,3,9,5%) →10.5`. Coverage comes from selected product; surface preparation and primer are outside scope.
- **Flooring**: `ceil(room L × W × (1+w/100)/pack m²)`, output whole packs. Round only after the allowance. Fixtures `(5,4,2,10%) →11`; `(3,3,2,0%) →5`; `(2,2,3,5%) →2`.
- **Roofing**: consistent pitched roof, horizontal rectangular footprint in metres including any desired overhang. `L × W × sqrt(1+(rise/run)²) × (1+w/100)`. Rise is nonnegative, run positive, both bounded to one million. Same-unit pitch is dimensionless. Decimal sqrt at 50 digits; independent irrational references use relative/absolute tolerance 1e-12. Fixtures footprint 10×8: flat →80m²; rise/run 3/4 →100m²; same pitch with 10% allowance →110m². No structural sizing, valleys, dormers or differing pitch sections.
- **Tile**: `ceil(net m² × 10000 × (1+w/100)/(width cm × height cm))`, output whole tiles. Fixtures `(10,50,50,10%) →44`; `(1,25,25,0%) →16`; `(1,30,30,0%) →12`. Ignores grout joints, layout and tiles per box; a layout takeoff may differ.
- **Lumber**: actual thickness and width in inches, length in feet, integer number of boards. `T × W × L × Q /12`, output board feet (one board foot =144in³). Fixtures `(1,12,8,1) →8`; `(1.5,3.5,8,4) →14`; `(2,6,10,2) →20`. Nominal lumber dimensions, grade, species and strength are not inferred.

Fixtures are independently authored, including upward purchase rounding; malformed/exponent/grouped/zero dimensions fail. All lessons document allowances and exclusions. Video sources exist; rendering and visual inspection remain pending.
