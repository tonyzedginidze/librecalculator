# Cooking numerical contracts

All engines use the shared 50-significant-digit Decimal context and canonical decimal bounds (256 characters, 30 significant digits, adjusted exponent magnitude at most 100). No intermediate presentation rounding. Failures explicitly identify malformed fields, domain violations or output overflow. Repeating division uses absolute-plus-relative tolerance 1e-12; terminating examples are checked exactly as well.

- Recipe scaling: quantity is nonnegative and keeps its supplied ingredient unit. Original and target yields are positive, possibly fractional. Forward Q′=Q×target/original, factor=target/original, reverse Q=Q′×original/target. Timing, equipment, salt preferences and fermentation do not scale automatically.
- Cup/gram conversion: use a strictly positive user-supplied grams-per-cup calibration. The chosen cup size and filling technique belong to this calibration; no universal ingredient density is claimed. Nonnegative cups×calibration yields grams, grams/calibration yields cups, calibration/2 yields half-cup grams.
- Oven temperature: F=C×9/5+32, C=(F−32)×5/9. Signed temperatures are valid at or above exact absolute zero −273.15 °C or −459.67 °F. Fan adjustment subtracts an entered nonnegative Celsius reduction from a Celsius conventional setting and rejects adjusted values below absolute zero. Reduction is a recipe/appliance assumption, not a physical conversion constant.
- Baking ratios: positive flour grams; nonnegative ingredient grams and baker percentages. Percentage=ingredient/flour×100, ingredient=flour×percentage/100, flour=ingredient×100/percentage. The inverse percentage divisor must be positive. Percentages above 100 are supported; totals need not sum to 100.
- Calories: nonnegative kcal totals/per-serving kcal, positive serving count (fractional allowed), positive total cooked grams, nonnegative portion grams. Per-serving=total/servings; recipe=per-serving×servings; weight portion=total×portionGrams/totalGrams. Portions can represent multiple recipe batches and are not clamped to total mass. Estimates presume homogeneous food composition and supplied accurate nutrition totals.

Independent examples: quantity 250 from 4 to 12 servings gives 750, scale factor 3 and reverse 250; calibration 120 g/cup converts 2 cups to 240 g, 240 g to 2 cups and half cup to 60 g; 180 °C ↔ 356 °F, with a chosen 20 °C reduction giving fan 160 °C; 325 g ingredient/500 g flour gives 65%, reversed to 325 g ingredient or 500 g flour; 1,000 kcal divided four ways gives 250 kcal, reversed gives 1,000 kcal, and 200 g of a homogeneous 800 g/1,000 kcal batch gives 250 kcal.

References: NIST SP 811 measurement conventions (https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811), USDA FoodData Central for supplied nutrition totals (https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/). No supplied ingredient calibration or oven reduction is presented as an authoritative universal value.
